Solutions

Simulation services built on rigorous first-principles models

Every engagement starts from a validated model of your plant. From there we answer the questions that carry real operational and financial weight — safely, and before you commit to hardware or a shutdown.

01

Steady-State Simulation

Aspen HYSYSAspen PlusPRO/IIPetroSIMUniSim

In today's competitive industrial landscape an accurate heat and mass balance is not just a technical document — it is the foundation of cost-effective design, safe operations and long-term profitability. We build high-fidelity models that go far beyond basic calculations, transforming raw process data into actionable engineering insight so you can optimise energy consumption, eliminate bottlenecks and cut capital and operating expense before spending a dollar on hardware.

02

Dynamic Simulation

Aspen HYSYSUniSimAspen Adsim

Your plant doesn't operate at a single steady-state point — it experiences start-ups, trips and continuous fluctuations. Relying only on static calculations leaves you blind to critical operational risk. Our dynamic simulation services model the true transient behaviour of your process, letting us virtually test control narratives, emergency shutdown sequences and compressor anti-surge systems. The result is the confidence to make critical operational decisions — improving safety, protecting assets and avoiding costly downtime.

03

Operator Training Simulators

Aspen HYSYSUniSimJavaPython

Your plant never truly runs at steady state, and neither should your training. Static procedures don't prepare operators for the chaos of real-world upsets. Our OTS delivers a safe virtual environment with a fully replicated DCS and SIS, featuring a similar HMI — so every screen, alarm and control button looks and behaves exactly like the real console. Operators train on start-ups, trips and emergency scenarios repeatedly, without risking the plant. The result is a workforce that responds with confidence, reduces human error and prevents costly downtime.

04

Digital Twin

Aspen HYSYSUniSimJavaPython

Connected online with the real plant instrumentation, the process digital twin becomes a Real-Time Simulator — a live digital mirror of your operating asset that continuously calculates pressures, temperatures, flows and compositions for every stream and every tray in every column. It acts as a soft sensor and backup for online analysers, provides advanced bad-actor detection through holistic alarms such as "compressor degraded efficiency" instead of single physical instruments, and delivers look-ahead predictions by running faster than real time — forecasting events like "high-pressure column will flood in 10 minutes" or "product quality will go off-spec in 30 minutes". Always synchronised to current plant state, it doubles as a what-if advisor, and with autosaved snapshots it enables post-incident analysis by restoring exact past conditions and replaying the scenario.

05

Real-Time Optimisation

Aspen HYSYSUniSimPython

Your plant never truly runs at its optimal point — feed quality changes, ambient conditions shift, equipment degrades and market prices fluctuate, and static design calculations can't keep up. Our RTO services use a high-fidelity, rigorous process model that continuously calculates the optimal operating conditions for your plant — maximising yield, minimising energy consumption and improving profitability — while respecting all process constraints, equipment limits and safety margins. The RTO delivers constraint-aware recommendations, enables what-if analysis before changes reach the real plant, and uncovers debottlenecking opportunities by quantifying the financial impact of removing process limitations.

06

Fault Diagnostics

Aspen HYSYSUniSimPython

Your plant's critical faults — compressor surge, heat exchanger fouling, reactor runaway, valve sticking, pump cavitation, column flooding and instrumentation drift — often go undetected until they cause failure. Our fault detection and diagnosis services combine first-principles process models with machine learning (classification, PCA, PLS, neural networks) and real-time SCADA/DCS data to detect specific fault signatures, isolate faulty components, classify fault severity and trigger predictive alerts before operational limits are exceeded — eliminating reliance on manual log reviews or single-variable threshold alarms.

Real applications

  • Compressor vibration — frequency analysis and amplitude trends detecting bearing wear, impeller imbalance and surge precursors.
  • Heat exchanger fouling — fouling factor and U-value tracked from inlet/outlet temperatures and duty, scheduling cleaning before production cuts.
  • Pump cavitation — suction pressure drop, motor current spikes and vibration pattern changes, avoiding impeller erosion.
  • Column flooding — DP trends, tray temperature profiles and liquid level oscillations, preventing off-spec product.
  • Instrument drift — cross-correlation of redundant sensors and model-based validation, without manual recalibration.
07

Soft Sensors

Aspen HYSYSUniSimPython

Your plant never runs with full visibility — critical variables such as composition, quality and efficiency are often unmeasured or too expensive to monitor. Our soft sensor services use rigorous process models and real-time data to infer these variables continuously, eliminating the need for laboratory tests, reducing human error and allowing safe monitoring of toxic or hazardous streams — so you can improve quality and reduce costs without installing expensive hardware.

Real applications

  • Air Separation Unit — real-time O₂ and N₂ purity inference from column temperatures, pressures and flows, backing up costly analysers.
  • Wastewater treatment — online BOD, COD, dissolved oxygen, pH, SVI and total nitrogen/phosphorus inferred from process data.
  • Water unit — online silica, conductivity, TDS and resin breakthrough from flow, temperature and bed age, replacing grab samples and manual resin testing.

Training

Process Engineering Specialised Courses

Industry-focused, hands-on programmes delivered by practising engineers and academics — taught on the same software used in operating plants. Twelve courses, 276 hours of instruction, covering simulation, equipment design, engineering programming and applied AI.

01

Steady-State Simulation with Aspen HYSYS

Aspen HYSYS24 hours

Comprehensive training on steady-state process simulation using Aspen HYSYS, covering flow sheet development, unit operations (distillation, heat exchangers, reactors, compressors, pumps), fluid package selection, oil characterisation, hypo component generation, crude oil blending, recycle convergence, column hydraulic rating, reactor kinetics, sensitivity analysis, optimisation, spreadsheet use and validation.

02

Dynamic Simulation with Aspen HYSYS

Aspen HYSYS24 hours

Comprehensive training on dynamic simulation using Aspen HYSYS, covering dynamic model setup from a steady-state basis, unit operations (distillation, heat exchangers, reactors, compressors, pumps), pressure-flow network configuration, control system design and tuning (PID controllers), safety shutdown and interlock systems, disturbance analysis and scenario testing, dynamic column and reactor behaviour, compressor surge analysis and anti-surge control, emergency depressurisation studies, controller performance validation and validation against plant operational data.

03

Relief Load Calculation & Overpressure Protection

Aspen HYSYS — Safety Analysis24 hours

Comprehensive training on relief load calculation and overpressure protection, covering relief system design principles, identification of overpressure scenarios (blocked outlet, fire exposure, thermal expansion, cooling failure, power failure, instrument failure, reflux failure), API 520/521/526 standards, pressure relief device selection (PSV, PRV, rupture discs), PSV sizing and orifice calculation, rupture disc selection and application, inlet and outlet piping configuration and pressure drop analysis, and depressurisation and blowdown system design.

04

Tank, Vessel & Tower Design

Koch-Glitsch · Excel30 hours

Comprehensive training on the process design of storage tanks — atmospheric and pressurised, including fixed roof, floating roof and spherical tanks — covering tank selection, sizing, overpressure protection and blanketing; the process design of pressure vessels, covering vertical and horizontal vessels including two-phase and three-phase separators, with vessel sizing, process internals selection and overpressure protection; and the process design of towers, covering trayed and packed towers with tower sizing, hydraulic design, tray and packing selection, process internals, stage efficiency, flooding and entrainment analysis and pressure drop calculations.

05

Flare Network Design with FlareNet & FlareSim

Aspen FlareNet · FlareSim18 hours

Comprehensive training on flare network and stack design, covering tail pipe sizing, sub-header and header sizing, back pressure calculations, flare location determination, knock-out drum sizing, flare pump sizing, radiation analysis, and API 521 & API 537 compliance.

06

Process Diagram Development — PFD & P&ID

AutoCAD24 hours

Comprehensive training on the development and interpretation of PFD, P&ID and UFD, covering project specifications, legend and symbols, IPS-E-PR-170 and IPS-E-PR-230, PFD components, heat and material balance, equipment data requirements (heat exchanger, furnace, reactor, column, pump, compressor, tank), instrumentation and control systems, line information and PMS, HYSYS line sizing, shutdown levels, UFD development, and case studies for gas and oil refinery units.

07

Programming for Process Engineering

Python · MATLAB · gPROMS30 hours

Comprehensive training covering Python programming for data analysis and visualisation, MATLAB for numerical computing and algorithm development, and gPROMS for steady-state and dynamic process modelling. Includes model development for unit operations (reactors, distillation columns, heat exchangers, pumps, compressors, crystallisers, bioreactors), parameter estimation, process optimisation, heat and material balance, equipment data requirements, instrumentation and control systems, and dynamic optimisation for batch and continuous processes. Features case studies for oil and gas, petrochemical and pharmaceutical bioprocesses, along with integration workflows combining Python, MATLAB and gPROMS for end-to-end simulation and analysis.

08

Adsorption Column Design with Aspen Adsim

Aspen Adsim12 hours

Comprehensive training on simulation of PSA, VSA and fixed-bed adsorption using Aspen Adsim, covering component/adsorbent definition, equilibrium isotherms, mass transfer coefficients (LDF), mass and energy balances, cycle simulation, configuration, bed interactions, cyclic steady state determination, breakthrough curve analysis, and industrial case studies including air separation units.

09

Air Cooler Design with HTRI

HTRI24 hours

Comprehensive training on air cooler design and simulation, covering thermal design, hydraulic design, fin tube selection, fan selection (forced draft / induced draft), tube bundle configuration, header design, air-side and process-side modelling, pressure drop calculations, vibration analysis, performance rating, troubleshooting and optimisation.

10

Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger Design with HTRI

HTRI24 hours

Comprehensive training on the thermal and hydraulic design of shell & tube heat exchangers using HTRI, covering TEMA types and nomenclature, shell and tube layout, tube count and bundle configuration, baffle and support design, shell-side and tube-side heat transfer and pressure drop modelling, fouling factors and margins, vibration analysis, rating and debottlenecking of existing exchangers, and integration with rigorous process simulation.

Steady-State Simulation with Aspen Plus
